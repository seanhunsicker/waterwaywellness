The Waterway Wellness Run Club, often called the "People's Run Club," has built a massive following in Fort Lauderdale by embracing a refreshingly honest motto: "Running Sucks."Rather than focusing on competitive times or marathons, they prioritize social connection, mental health, and community vibes.Core Schedule & EventsMondays at 6:00 PM: The main event. A casual 2-mile run or walk starting at Laura Ward Plaza in downtown Fort Lauderdale.Two-Mile Tuesdays: Hosted at Tarpon River Brewing at 6:30 PM. The first 35 runners often receive a free beer, making it a highly popular social mixer.Weekend Pop-ups: They frequently host "Hype & Zen" events, combining 2-mile runs with free sound bath classes, ice bath parties, and yoga sessions.Why People Love ItNon-Runner Friendly: Many members describe themselves as "non-runners" who show up strictly for the people and the atmosphere.Major Giveaways: The club is known for giving back, frequently awarding free tickets to events like the Miami Open or concerts (e.g., The Kid LAROI) to participants.High Energy: Events often feature live DJs (like The Unxpctd) and partnerships with brands like Red Bull and Imprint Supplements to keep the energy high.Networking: It is widely cited as one of the best ways to make friends in Fort Lauderdale, bridging the gap between a standard workout and a social club.How to JoinThere is no formal membership fee or long-term commitment.
